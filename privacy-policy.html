01

Who We Are

Astra Digital is an e-commerce solutions company that helps businesses build, operate and grow their digital commerce presence.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact us using the details provided at the end of this policy.

02

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name

  • Your email address
  • Your phone number and country code
  • Information about your business or requirements
03

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Understand your business requirements
  • Discuss our services with you
  • Arrange a strategy call or consultation
  • Follow up regarding your enquiry

04

How Your Information Is Stored

Information submitted through our website contact form is transmitted to our form processing system and recorded in a Google Sheet used to manage enquiries.

A notification may also be sent to our designated business email address when a new enquiry is submitted.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

05

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers that help us operate our website, manage enquiries, store information or communicate with you.

We may also disclose information where required to comply with applicable law or a lawful request.

06

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including communication, business administration and applicable legal or contractual requirements.

07

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of information we hold about you.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
