FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What exactly does Astra Digital handle? +

We handle the parts of eCommerce that keep a store moving from Shopify and store setup to operations, tracking, performance marketing, SEO, WhatsApp, customer support and ongoing optimization.

Do you work with existing stores or only new brands? +

Both. We work with existing stores that need better systems, performance or growth, as well as brands building their eCommerce operation from the ground up.

Do I need to hire different people for different services? +

No. Astra is built to work as one connected team across store, operations and growth, so you don't have to coordinate multiple agencies for different parts of the business.

Who will I actually be working with? +

You work directly with the people handling your account. There are no unnecessary account-manager layers or junior handoffs between you and the team doing the work.

How do you decide what my brand needs? +

We start by understanding how your store currently works including the storefront, catalog, tracking, operations and growth channels. From there, we identify what needs fixing, improving or scaling.

Can Astra work with us long-term? +

Yes. We're built around ongoing partnerships rather than one-off projects. As your store grows, we can continue handling and improving the systems behind it.
